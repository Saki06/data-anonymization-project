COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS: HIGH-RISK vs LOW-RISK RECORDS
============================================================

High-risk records have higher values in max_attack_score and mean_attack_score. This means they are more likely to face threats. The pair_count may also be higher, indicating more connections that could lead to risks. 

Low-risk records show lower values in max_attack_score and mean_attack_score. They are less likely to be targeted. The pair_count is usually lower, suggesting fewer connections that could create risks.
