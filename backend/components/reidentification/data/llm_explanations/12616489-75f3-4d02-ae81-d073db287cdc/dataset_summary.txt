DATASET-LEVEL PRIVACY RISK SUMMARY
============================================================

This dataset has a high overall privacy risk. There are 80 records in total. Out of these, 72 records, or 90.0%, are considered high risk. Only 4 records are medium risk, and 4 records are low risk. This means most of the data is at a high risk of being linked back to individuals.

The columns that matter most for risk are age, birth_year, sex, marital_status, education, religion, and ethnicity. Age and birth_year are particularly important because they can easily identify someone. Sex, marital_status, education, religion, and ethnicity add more details. When combined, these columns can create a clearer picture of a person, increasing the risk.

To reduce privacy risk, some columns need stronger anonymization. For example, age could be grouped into ranges instead of exact numbers. Categories for marital_status, education, and religion could be broadened to include fewer specific options. Rare values in these columns should be removed or combined with larger groups. This will help protect individual identities better.

Risk scores represent ML model confidence under simulated attack conditions, not real-world re-identification probability.
