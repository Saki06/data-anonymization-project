DATASET-LEVEL PRIVACY RISK SUMMARY
============================================================

This dataset has a high overall privacy risk. Out of 85 records, 81 are considered high risk, which is about 95.3%. Only 2 records are medium risk and 2 are low risk. This means most records have a significant chance of being linked back to individuals.

The columns that matter most for risk are age, education, marital_status, and sex. Age is the biggest concern, as it can easily narrow down a person's identity. Education, marital_status, and sex also play a role. When these columns are combined, they create a clearer picture of who someone might be, increasing the risk.

To protect privacy better, the columns need stronger anonymization. For age, consider grouping ages into ranges, like 20-29 or 30-39. For education, broaden categories to general levels, such as high school or college. Marital_status could be simplified to just married or not married. Removing rare values can also help. These steps can make it harder to identify individuals while keeping the data useful. 

Risk scores represent ML model confidence under simulated attack conditions, not real-world re-identification probability.
