DATASET-LEVEL PRIVACY RISK SUMMARY
============================================================

The dataset has 1000 records. All of them are considered medium risk. There are no high or low risk records. This means that every record has some level of privacy concern.

The columns that matter most for risk are industry, education, age, sex, and marital_status. Each of these columns can provide important details about a person. When combined, they can create a clearer picture of someone's identity. This increases the chance of someone being linked to their data.

To improve privacy, the columns need stronger anonymization. For example, ages could be grouped into ranges like 20-30 or 30-40. Categories for industry and education could be broadened to include fewer specific types. Removing rare values can also help. These steps can make it harder to connect data back to individuals. 

Risk scores represent ML model confidence under simulated attack conditions, not real-world re-identification probability.
